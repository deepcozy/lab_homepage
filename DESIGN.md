# Cozie homepage

Recreate the supplied warm, spacious Cozie introduction with dark geometric typography and the large pink cloud anchored to the lower right.

## Design source

- [Figma homepage, frame 9:14](https://www.figma.com/design/HiiOTYn7wU4dTd2g4Jjt0L/Cozei-Official-Web?node-id=9-14)
- Reference canvas: 1920 × 1080.
- Palette: warm white `#FDFAF9`, ink `#0A1227`, scan corners `#9FA1A7`.
- Typography: Outfit variable font, weights 400 and 700. Font files are self-hosted; see `assets/fonts/OFL.txt` for their SIL Open Font License.
- Reference type sizes: wordmark 52.258px, headline 126.29px, subtitle 39.194px, scan label 30.484px.
- Desktop composition: wordmark at (99.07, 58.79), introduction at (106.69, 290.69), scan row at (113.23, 590.08), cloud at (895, 228), size 1025 × 852.
- Cloud PNG and scan-corner SVG are downloaded directly from this Figma frame. The SVG's root dimensions and paths are preserved.

## Responsive adaptation

Desktop follows the Figma composition. Typography scales with viewport width; the cloud stays at the lower right with its design aspect ratio. At 700px and below, and on portrait tablet viewports up to 1100px wide, content stacks above the cloud so the text remains readable. Vertical scrolling is allowed on small screens.

## Content and behavior

Keep the three supplied text lines and wordmark. The white scan area is an empty QR placeholder in Figma; no destination URL was supplied, so it stays a non-interactive placeholder. There are no invented links, product claims, animations or extra sections.
